# Investor Story Embedded in the Website

## Purpose

The marketing site should make the investment thesis understandable without turning the homepage into a pitch deck.

## Narrative

### Problem

Businesses still operate through fragmented human workflows across calls, inboxes, documents, spreadsheets, CRMs, accounting systems, and approvals.

Generative AI alone does not complete those workflows.

### Entry wedge

Optimus starts with five concrete products:

**Customer/operations**
- Voice
- Lead
- Office

**Finance**
- AR
- Analyst

These products target workflows with measurable operational or financial value.

### Platform thesis

Repeated product capabilities can converge into a shared layer:

- identity and permissions
- business context
- agent orchestration
- integrations
- approval engine
- observability
- security
- model abstraction
- workflow intelligence

### Expansion thesis

Once the foundation and customer distribution exist, Optimus can expand into adjacent workflows.

Finance examples:
- AP
- Close
- Cash

Business examples:
- Documents
- Support
- Sales

### Defensibility hypothesis

Potential defensibility should come from:
- deeply embedded workflows
- integrations
- domain-specific product behavior
- customer configuration
- operational data/feedback loops where permitted
- trust/security
- switching costs
- product distribution

Do not claim these moats already exist.

## Website implementation

The homepage should communicate:

**Focused now → platform later.**

Avoid investor buzzwords such as:
- revolutionary
- world-changing
- AGI
- category-defining

unless there is substance behind them.

## Traction

When traction exists, add a factual section containing verified metrics.

Until then use:
- product status
- design-partner invitation
- founder/company mission
- live demos

Never manufacture traction.
