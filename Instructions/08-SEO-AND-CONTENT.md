# SEO and Content Strategy

## SEO objective

Build authority around practical AI operations rather than chasing generic terms such as "AI company."

## Initial topic clusters

### AI Voice / Home Services
- AI receptionist for HVAC companies
- AI answering service for contractors
- missed calls in home services
- AI lead qualification for service businesses

### Business Automation
- AI workflow automation
- AI agents for small and mid-sized businesses
- AI email workflow automation
- AI operations automation

### Finance AI
- AI for accounts receivable
- accounts receivable automation
- AI collections workflow
- AI financial analysis
- AI variance analysis
- AI for controllers and CFOs

## Technical SEO

Implement:
- unique metadata per page
- canonical URLs
- Open Graph metadata
- Twitter/X card metadata where appropriate
- sitemap.xml
- robots.txt
- structured data where valid
- Organization schema
- Article schema for real articles
- Breadcrumb schema where useful

Do not abuse schema.

## Local positioning

Mention Calgary naturally in:
- About
- Contact
- footer/company description

Do not turn the entire website into a local SEO site if the ambition is broader than Calgary.

Preferred phrase:

**Built in Calgary. Designed for businesses everywhere.**

## Content quality

Do not publish large volumes of low-value AI-generated content.

Every article should:
- solve a specific problem
- contain examples
- demonstrate expertise
- connect naturally to an Optimus product
- avoid unsupported statistics
