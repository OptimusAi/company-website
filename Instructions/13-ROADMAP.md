# Optimus AI Roadmap

This roadmap separates the **website roadmap** from the **product roadmap**.

## Stage 1 — Marketing foundation

### Website
- premium homepage
- five product pages
- Platform
- Security
- About
- Contact
- Design Partner program
- analytics
- SEO
- deploy

### Business objective
Generate customer discovery conversations and early pilot leads.

## Stage 2 — Product demonstrations

Build credible interactive demonstrations for:

### Voice
Simulated call/transcript/qualification/booking.

### Lead
Simulated lead conversation and qualification.

### Office
Workflow simulation.

### AR
Interactive sample aging dashboard and collections workflow.

### Analyst
Sample financial dataset with guided questions and safe precomputed/demo answers.

Clearly identify simulated/sample data.

## Stage 3 — First live product

Recommended initial commercial candidate:
**Optimus Voice**, subject to customer validation.

Goals:
- first pilot customers
- measure call handling
- qualification
- appointments
- human handoff
- reliability

## Stage 4 — Lead product

Reuse common capabilities:
- communication
- workflow orchestration
- CRM integrations
- scheduling

## Stage 5 — Finance prototype

### Optimus AR
Start with controlled imports such as anonymized/sample AR-aging data before deep ERP integrations.

Capabilities:
- aging analysis
- collection prioritization
- workflow statuses
- draft follow-up
- blocker categorization
- dashboard

## Stage 6 — Analyst prototype

Start with structured uploads/sample data:
- GL
- budget
- actuals
- AR
- AP

Build:
- deterministic metrics
- variance analysis
- conversational exploration
- traceable sources

## Stage 7 — Platform extraction

Only after repeated patterns emerge, extract common platform capabilities:

- identity
- organizations/tenants
- permissions
- integrations
- agent runtime
- workflow orchestration
- approval engine
- audit logs
- observability
- usage/metering
- model gateway

## Stage 8 — Expansion

Potential future finance modules:
- AP
- Close
- Cash

Potential broader modules:
- Documents
- Support
- Sales

Do not build these merely to make the product menu larger. Add modules when customer evidence supports them.
