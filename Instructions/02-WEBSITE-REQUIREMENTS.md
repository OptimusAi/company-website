# Optimus AI Website Requirements

## Website goals

The website has four primary jobs:

1. Acquire potential customers.
2. Recruit design partners/pilot customers.
3. Establish Optimus as a credible AI product company.
4. Communicate a scalable platform story to potential investors and strategic partners.

## Primary audiences

### Business owners / operations leaders
Need to quickly understand how Optimus can capture leads and automate repetitive work.

### CFOs / controllers / finance leaders
Need credible, controlled finance automation and intelligence.

### Investors
Need to see:
- focused entry wedge
- coherent product architecture
- scalable platform thesis
- credible execution
- market expansion potential

### Future employees
Need to understand mission and ambition.

## Homepage structure

### 1. Navigation
Premium, minimal, sticky after scrolling.

### 2. Hero
Headline:

**AI that does the work.**

Supporting text:

**Intelligent AI agents for customer operations and finance.**

Expanded copy can explain calls, leads, workflows, receivables, and financial analysis.

Primary CTA:
**Book a Demo**

Secondary CTA:
**Explore Optimus**

Hero should include a custom animated product/system visualization.

### 3. Problem / transformation statement

Headline:

**From repetitive work to intelligent operations.**

Explain that businesses spend significant employee time answering calls, following up, moving information, collecting receivables, and analyzing financial data.

### 4. Product portfolio

Show two families:

**Optimus Business**
- Voice
- Lead
- Office

**Optimus Finance**
- AR
- Analyst

Every card needs:
- short outcome-oriented headline
- one-paragraph explanation
- status label
- Learn More link

### 5. Optimus Voice showcase

Visual demo of:
incoming call → intent detection → qualification → appointment booking → business notification.

### 6. Optimus Lead / Office showcase

Show automated lead qualification and workflow orchestration.

### 7. Optimus Finance section

Use a stronger enterprise visual treatment.

Headline:

**Intelligence for modern finance teams.**

Show AR dashboard and Analyst conversational financial analysis.

### 8. Platform

Headline:

**One intelligence layer. Multiple AI agents.**

Illustrate:
data + context + agents + workflows + integrations + human approval.

### 9. Industries / solutions

Show:
- Home Services
- Construction
- Professional Services
- Finance Teams

### 10. How it works

Three-stage story:

**Connect → Understand → Act**

Add human approval/governance where required.

### 11. Trust/security

Communicate principles:
- controlled access
- data protection
- auditability
- human review
- responsible AI

Do not claim certifications that do not exist.

### 12. Vision / investor narrative

Headline:

**We're building the intelligence layer for business operations.**

Show evolution:

Today:
Voice / Lead / Office / AR / Analyst

Future opportunities:
AP / Close / Cash / Documents / Support

End-state:
Optimus AI Operations Platform

Clearly label future products as roadmap concepts.

### 13. Design Partner CTA

Headline:

**Build the future of work with us.**

Explain that Optimus is working with selected businesses to develop and validate workflows.

CTA:
**Become a Design Partner**

### 14. Insights

Display latest real articles when available.

### 15. Final CTA

**Put AI to work in your business.**

Buttons:
- Book a Demo
- Explore Products

### 16. Footer

Columns:
Products / Solutions / Platform / Resources / Company / Legal

Footer positioning:
**Built in Calgary. Designed for businesses everywhere.**

- Lead — Private Pilot
- Office — Early Access
