# Optimus AI — Product Vision

## Company

**Name:** Optimus AI  
**Location:** Calgary, Alberta, Canada  
**Website:** optimus-ai.com

## Mission

Make intelligent automation accessible to businesses by building AI agents that understand business context, coordinate workflows, and help execute real operational work.

## Positioning

### Primary headline

# AI that does the work.

### Supporting positioning

Deploy intelligent AI agents across customer operations and finance — answering calls, converting leads, automating workflows, improving receivables operations, and helping finance teams make faster decisions.

## Market entry strategy

Optimus should not begin as a foundation-model company.

It should use leading AI models and infrastructure while owning the higher-value application layer:

- business workflows
- domain-specific agents
- integrations
- customer experience
- financial intelligence
- permissions and controls
- proprietary workflow data and product knowledge over time

## Initial portfolio

### Optimus Business

#### Optimus Voice
AI-powered customer conversations and call handling.

#### Optimus Lead
AI-powered lead engagement, qualification, follow-up, and conversion.

#### Optimus Office
AI-powered repetitive business workflow automation.

### Optimus Finance

#### Optimus AR
AI-powered accounts-receivable intelligence and collections workflows.

#### Optimus Analyst
AI-powered financial analysis, variance investigation, reporting, and business questions.

## Strategic narrative

### Today
Solve clear, expensive workflows with five focused products.

### Next
Reuse common platform capabilities across products.

### Future
Become an AI operations platform through which organizations deploy, manage, govern, and measure AI agents across business functions.

## Commercial model

Potential early pricing model:

- implementation/setup fee
- monthly recurring platform/service fee
- usage component where appropriate
- custom enterprise pricing later

Pricing shown on the website should be omitted unless explicitly approved. Prefer **Book a Demo** and **Become a Design Partner** while products are early.

## Principles

1. Solve measurable business problems.
2. Human control for consequential workflows.
3. Integrate rather than force customers to replace core systems.
4. Demonstrate ROI.
5. Start narrow and productize repeated patterns.
6. Security and permissions are platform capabilities, not afterthoughts.
7. Never exaggerate product maturity.
