## Header behavior

Desktop:
- logo left
- primary navigation center/left
- CTA right

Mobile:
- logo
- menu trigger
- full-screen or high-quality drawer
- Book a Demo remains easy to reach

## CTA rules

Primary CTA:
**Book a Demo**

Secondary:
**Explore Products**

Pilot CTA:
**Become a Design Partner**

Avoid having five competing CTA styles.

## URL naming

Use lowercase, human-readable routes.

Do not use internal project codenames in public URLs.
