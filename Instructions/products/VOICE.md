# Product Specification — Optimus Voice

## Positioning

**Every call answered. Every opportunity captured.**

Optimus Voice is an AI-powered customer conversation product designed initially for service businesses.

## Initial target markets

- HVAC
- plumbing
- electrical
- roofing
- landscaping
- renovation
- other appointment-driven services

## Customer problem

Businesses:
- miss after-hours calls
- interrupt employees to answer repetitive questions
- lose customer details
- respond inconsistently
- manually schedule appointments
- fail to capture lead context

## Core workflow

```text
Incoming call
→ AI answers
→ intent identified
→ customer details captured
→ qualification
→ urgency assessed
→ approved next action
→ appointment/request created
→ confirmation
→ business notified
→ CRM/system updated where integrated
```

## MVP capabilities

- inbound voice conversation
- configurable greeting
- intent classification
- caller information capture
- business-specific FAQ/context
- qualification questions
- urgency rules
- appointment request/booking integration
- SMS/email confirmation where configured
- human transfer/escalation
- call summary
- transcript where legally/operationally appropriate
- admin configuration

## Safety/control

AI must not invent:
- prices
- appointment availability
- emergency instructions
- guarantees

Use configured business rules.

Emergency/safety scenarios should use explicit escalation rules.

## Website demo

Use simulated company and sample data.

Example:

**Customer:** My furnace stopped working.

Show:
- Service: Furnace repair
- Urgency: High
- Location: Calgary NW
- Customer captured
- Appointment request created

Do not imply a real customer interaction.

## Future capabilities

- outbound follow-up
- multilingual support
- advanced routing
- CRM integrations
- call analytics
- quality review
- multi-location routing

## Success metrics

- answered-call rate
- qualified leads
- appointments/request completions
- handoff rate
- call containment where appropriate
- customer/business satisfaction
