# Product Specification — Optimus Lead

## Positioning

**Turn inquiries into opportunities.**

Optimus Lead is designed to engage inbound leads quickly, collect qualification information, follow up, and move qualified opportunities toward a human sales process or appointment.

## Initial customers

- home services
- renovation
- construction
- contractors
- professional services

## Problem

Inbound leads often:
- wait hours for a response
- receive inconsistent qualification
- are not followed up
- lack structured information
- disappear between website, inbox, CRM, and scheduling

## Workflow

```text
Lead submitted
→ immediate approved-channel response
→ qualification
→ structured lead profile
→ routing
→ appointment/consultation
→ follow-up sequence
→ CRM update
→ human escalation
```

## MVP

- website lead ingestion
- SMS/email engagement where consent/rules permit
- configurable qualification flows
- structured lead profile
- lead status
- scheduling
- follow-up workflow
- human takeover
- CRM-ready payload

## Example qualification

For a renovation lead:

- project type
- approximate size
- location
- desired timeline
- budget range if appropriate
- preferred consultation time

## Website demo

Use clearly fictional data.

Show:
- new lead
- conversation
- qualification fields populating
- status changing to Qualified
- consultation booked

## Controls

- configurable message templates/policies
- frequency limits
- opt-out handling
- no unsupported pricing promises
- human escalation

## Success metrics

- speed to first response
- qualification completion
- appointment conversion
- lead-to-opportunity conversion
- follow-up completion
